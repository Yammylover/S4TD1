Equation field

Lambda

Lambda is a mixed fighter. Trained to the use of his staff, he can pair offense and defense, but lacks any kind of magical power.

Omega

Omega is a defensive support. He can take strong blows, and heal his allies.

Sigma

Sigma compensate his physical weakness by summoning allies. He can summon mindless copies to attack, or temporary copies to use the ennemy's skill against them.

Hallownest

Knight

The Knight is a small bug, of unknow origin and intentions. He will be your best ally in the treacherous land of Hallownest.

Infected Swarm

A deadly swarm of bloodthirsty flies. They will take over nearby corpses to strenghten themselves.

The Hollow Knight

A pure vessel tasked to contain the scourge of Hallownest. Tainted by its creator, its failure led to Hallownest's fall.

Ender Dragon

The last ennemy standing between you and salvation. No remorse must slow you down.
